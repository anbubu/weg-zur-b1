# Weg zur B1

German from A1 to the Goethe-Zertifikat B1. Lessons, flashcards, games, reading, writing and exam practice. Works offline once installed, no account needed.

**Open the app:** https://anbubu.github.io/weg-zur-b1/

## Install it

Open the link once **with internet**. After that the app works with no internet at all.

- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap the **⋮** menu, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**), then **Install**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link in Safari, tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
- **Computer (Chrome or Edge):** open the link and click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the **⋮** menu and choose **Install Weg zur B1**.

## Move your progress between devices

Your progress is saved only on the device you use. To move it:

1. Tap the **gear** button at the top of the app (Settings).
2. Under *Copy my progress*, tap **Copy progress code** and send the code to yourself (WhatsApp, e-mail, notes).
3. On the other device, open the app, tap the gear, paste the code and tap **Replace this device’s progress**.

Copy your code now and then as a backup too: if a phone is lost or reset, the code brings everything back.

## Where to start

Open the app and press the big button on **Heute**. It always shows one next step. If you are new, it starts with a short lesson on how German sounds. The **Lernpfad** has seven stages, from your first greetings to the start of B1, then B1 exam training. **Fortschritt** shows your streak, weekly chart and weak spots.

## Gemini (optional)

The app works fully without it. With a free Gemini key it can also explain the mistakes in your writing, write new reading texts and write new exam sets (needs internet):

1. Open https://aistudio.google.com/apikey, sign in and create an API key.
2. In the app, tap the gear (Settings), paste the key under *Gemini* and tap **Save and test**.

The key stays on that device only and is not part of your progress code. Google's free plan may use what you send to improve its products, so don't write private things. Remove the key any time in Settings.

## A German voice for "read aloud"

The app reads German with your device's own voice, which works offline once a German voice is installed:

- **Android:** Settings → search **Text-to-speech** → Preferred engine: Google → ⚙ settings → **Install voice data** → Deutsch (Deutschland).
- **iPhone:** Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → **German** → download one.
- **Windows:** Settings → Time & language → Speech → **Add voices** → Deutsch.

## Uninstall

- **Android:** long-press the app icon, then **Uninstall**.
- **iPhone:** long-press the app icon, then **Remove App** → **Delete from Home Screen**.
- **Computer:** open the app, click **⋮** at the top right, then **Uninstall Weg zur B1**.

Uninstalling can delete your progress. Copy your progress code first if you might come back.

## Updates

When a new version is published here, the app picks it up the next time you open it with internet, and shows it the time after. Offline, it keeps working with the version you have.

## What needs internet

Only the YouTube links in the lessons. Everything else works offline.
